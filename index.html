    DESIGNED FOR THE REAL WORLD
    Trust is a product feature.
    Undo-First Agent is a portable safety layer for agents operating across files, CRM systems, calendars, subscriptions, and internal tools. It keeps the user in control without turning every task into a manual checklist.
    
          
    
            
    ↶
    Reversible by default
    Prefer moves, drafts, snapshots, and soft deletes over irreversible actions.

    Bounded authority
    Approval covers one declared plan with a limited scope and an expiry.
    
            
    ▤
    Evidence, not vibes
    Every action produces a receipt that explains what changed and how to recover.
